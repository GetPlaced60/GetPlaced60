👨‍💻 About Me
I'm a Senior DevOps Engineer focused on designing, operating, and improving infrastructure that teams can depend on. I enjoy solving real-world production problems, simplifying operational workflows, and building systems that are easier to monitor, secure, recover, and scale.
- ☁️ Building and maintaining cloud infrastructure with AWS.
- 🐳 Managing containerized workloads with Docker and Docker Swarm.
- 🔁 Working on high availability, disaster recovery, replication, and failover strategies.
- 📊 Improving observability with CloudWatch, metrics, alerts, and incident response workflows.
- 🔐 Strengthening application delivery and perimeter security with NGINX, WAF, and practical hardening.
- 🛠️ Troubleshooting production issues across Linux, networking, databases, and application infrastructure.
- 🤝 Working across teams to make deployments and day-to-day operations more reliable.
My mindset: automate the repeatable, monitor what matters, secure every layer, and always have a recovery plan.
